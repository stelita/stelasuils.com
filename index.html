Why me

Bias in AI starts as human bias. I work on both.

  • Human bias. Trained psychologist. Created ‘Debugging Bias’, a five-principle method for addressing unconscious bias in tech teams, and delivered DEI training and talks for Volkswagen Digital Solutions, Cloudflare, HP and the European Maritime Safety Agency.
  • Patterns of discrimination. DEI and intersectional analytics, and doctoral research on why women leave tech.
  • Fairness, from ethics to maths. I can help teams weigh the trade-offs between fairness definitions and what the EU AI Act requires. Built a bias engine that computes selection rates and impact ratios from aggregate counts, tested against the published tables of an NYC bias audit.
  • Human oversight. Building tools to measure bias and the effectiveness of human oversight in AI used for hiring, working from aggregate counts only.
  • Standards. CEN/CLC/JTC 21 (category A, industry): WG 3 Engineering aspects since 16 Oct 2026; WG 4 Foundational and societal aspects since 23 Oct 2026. National committee member, CTN-UNE 71/SC 42.
